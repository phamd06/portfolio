## 1st Lab Activity:
A workshop activity conducted with the primary focus of Privacy. Classroom was split into groups, in each group, each member had the possession of one secret number. Each member was tasked to share their own number to the other group members without actually revealing what that number was. My group concluded that for this specific issue, each member would possess 2 extra number of their choosing which they would add together to create a sum of numbers, then finally add their original secret numbers on top which when concluded, allowed for perfect secerecy and ultimately achieved the goal.


## 2nd Lab Activity:
A workshop activity conducted with the primary focus of Access Control. The classroom was split into groups, and in each group, 'rules' were created by the group members that each member inherited yet anybody outside the group did not. This demonstrated Access Control Lists as a concept, specifically authentication which narrates a person should be who they prove to be. After each group successfully decided their rules, they went on to go to other groups to figure out the other groups' rules. My group created three rules, firstly being that each member would wear a pen on their person whether discreetly noticeable or outright in the open, second being that all laptops that were present on the table were running Windows Operating System and lastly, each member of the group was wearing enclosed sneakers. These three rules were created with the intent of uniquely identifying the members of our group from other groups using these rules.
